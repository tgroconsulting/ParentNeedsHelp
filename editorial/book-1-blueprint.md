### The First 30 Days After Everything Changes

**Series:** When Your Parents Need You
**Status:** Editorial outline, Version 1.0

---

## Book positioning

**Target audience:** Adult children who have recently realized that one or both parents may need additional help, whether because of a sudden crisis or gradual changes.

**Central reader problem:** Something has changed, and the reader does not know where to begin, what matters most, or how much responsibility they should take on.

**Book promise:** By the end of this book, readers will understand how to organize the situation, identify immediate priorities, communicate with their parents and family, find appropriate resources, and create a manageable first-month plan.

**Important distinction:** This is not a medical or financial advice book, a dementia handbook, or a complete caregiving manual. It is a practical starting point.
