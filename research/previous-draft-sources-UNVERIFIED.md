# Sources and As-Of Dates

Every figure in this book that came from a website, report, or study is listed here by chapter. Each entry gives the publisher, the date of the source or the period its data covers, and a web address where one is available. All web sources were checked on November 1, 2026. Figures, program rules, and web addresses change, so check the current version before you rely on a number. Costs and programs also vary by state and by community.

## Chapter 1

- **Caregiver numbers and strain** (63 million caregivers, nearly one in four adults; 59 million caring for an adult; up about 20 million since 2015; nearly one in four giving 40 or more hours a week; one in five in fair or poor health; nearly half with a major financial impact). AARP and National Alliance for Caregiving, *Caregiving in the US 2025*. [AARP news release, July 24, 2025](https://www.aarp.org/press/releases/2025-07-24-new-report-reveals-crisis-point-for-americas-63-million-family-caregivers.html) and [AARP report page](https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/ltss/family-caregiving/family-caregiving-in-us-2025/).
- **Falls** (one in four adults 65 and older fall each year; leading cause of injury and injury death; age-adjusted fall death rate up 21% from 2018 to 2024). [CDC, Older Adult Falls Data and Research](https://www.cdc.gov/falls/data-research/index.html), page reviewed September 4, 2026, data through 2024.
- **Fall injuries and telling a provider** (about 37% injured; about half did not discuss the fall with a provider). [CDC, MMWR, September 23, 2016, "Falls and Fall Injuries Among Adults Aged ≥65 Years — United States, 2014"](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/65/wr/mm6537a2.htm). Data year 2014.
- **Delirium** (comes on over days; dementia over months to years; infections and medications are common causes). [VA Geriatrics and Extended Care, What Is Delirium?](https://www.va.gov/GERIATRICS/pages/delirium_what_is_delirium.asp) and [What Causes Delirium?](https://www.va.gov/Geriatrics/pages/delirium_what_causes_delirium.asp), both updated July 29, 2022.
- **Care costs** ($35 an hour; assisted living $74,400 a year; semi-private nursing home room $114,975 a year). [CareScout, 2025 Cost of Care Survey results](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260302283881/en/CareScout-Releases-2025-Cost-of-Care-Survey-Results), released March 2, 2026. Data collected July to November 2025. National medians.
- **Medicare and long-term care.** [Medicare.gov, Long-term care](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/long-term-care).
- **Eldercare Locator, 1-800-677-1116.** [Eldercare Locator, Administration for Community Living](https://eldercare.acl.gov), page modified March 2, 2026.

- **National Elder Fraud Hotline, 1-833-372-8311** (weekdays, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern). [U.S. Department of Justice, COPS Dispatch, September 2025](https://cops.usdoj.gov/html/dispatch/09-2025/elder_fraud_hotline.html) and [Office for Victims of Crime](https://ovc.ojp.gov/program/elder-fraud-abuse/national-elder-fraud-hotline).

- **Suicide rates** (men 75 and older, 40.1 per 100,000 in 2024). [CDC National Center for Health Statistics, Data Brief 572, published September 23, 2026](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db572.htm). Final 2024 data.
