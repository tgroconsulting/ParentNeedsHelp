## The three companion PDFs

**Caregiver\_Emergency\_Binder.pdf** (Chapter 8, Days 29–30). Twelve pages: Start Here, Emergency Quick Sheet, Medications, Doctors and Pharmacy, Insurance and Benefits, Legal Documents Location Sheet, Bills and Income, Expenses and Reimbursements Log, Backup Network, Accounts and Digital Access Index, Incident and Symptom Log, and the Monthly, Quarterly, and Yearly Maintenance Checklist.

**Weekly\_Care\_Dashboard\_and\_Emergency\_Handoff.pdf** (Chapter 9, Day 30 and every week after). Six pages: Weekly Care Dashboard, Appointment Prep and Visit Notes, Who Owns What, two pages of Decision Trees, and the Emergency Handoff Sheet.

**Care\_Transition\_Roadmap\_and\_90\_Day\_Plan.pdf** (Chapter 10, Days 31–90 and beyond). Nine pages: Stability Check and Re-Assessment Triggers, Increasing Help at Home, two hospital and rehab roadmaps, Care Options Compared, Prepare Without Deciding Too Soon, Protect the Caregiver, the 90-Day Family Action Plan, and a 60-minute Family Meeting Agenda.

**How to use them.** Type into the fields on screen, or print the pages and write by hand. Some web browsers display a PDF but won't save what you type, so if your entries disappear when you close the file, print it or open it in a desktop PDF reader. Keep a printed copy of the Emergency Quick Sheet on the fridge and another in your bag.
