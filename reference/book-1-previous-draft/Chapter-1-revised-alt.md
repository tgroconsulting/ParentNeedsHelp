**Your Parent Needs Help—Now What? A 30-Day Action Plan for Doctors, Documents, Home Safety, and Family Decisions**

# How This Book Works

This book guides you through the first 30 days of becoming your parent's care coordinator, followed by the next 60 days.

Each chapter has six consistent parts to ensure clarity and direction:

1. **The Frustration:** Identify what you are experiencing.

2. **What Is Happening:** Understand the underlying patterns.

3. **What to Do Now:** Follow concrete steps with specific day ranges.

4. **Exactly What to Say:** Access scripts for immediate use.

5. **A Tool:** Use checklists, worksheets, or printables for immediate application.

6. **When Professional Help Is Appropriate:** Know who to contact and when.

Start at Chapter 1 and follow the days, or jump to the chapter that addresses your current challenge. The days serve as a guide, not a deadline.
